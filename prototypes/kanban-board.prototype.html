Kanban board ↔ Gantt chart ↔ Prep view

Question: how does the kanban board record progress, and how does that progress relate to the Plan (Gantt chart) and the Prep view's checkboxes? Model being tested: progress lives on Steps only. A card is a Task; its column is derived from its Steps, so the board, the Prep view checkbox and the Gantt chart all show one state. Moving a card records what really happened (started at, hands-off at, done at). It never pins the Task. Real progress feeds the Plan: while planning it re-plans live, and from the start of Service day it only shows conflicts until the cook presses Re-plan. Simplified: no Availability or Equipment checks, only dependencies. Scenario: Saturday dinner, Service Sat 19:30, Cooks Malo and Sam.
