Unknown Step: which rule should the Plan read use?

The question. A Step can have active time null and passive time 0. The scheduler (Option A, the current shape.ts rule) counts that Step as unknown: 10 placeholder active minutes and the Task is approximate. The old TaskInfo rule (Option B, model.ts at 64960c7) counts it as 0 active, not approximate. The scheduler still places with Option A in both cases. Only what the Plan read reports differs: the Gantt bar, the due time (and so "behind"), and the ≈ flag. Which one should the Plan read use?

Free play edits one Task's Steps and times. Every change re-renders the state and both options side by side. Walkthrough tabs preload a scenario and then step through its buttons. Nothing is saved; reloading resets everything.
