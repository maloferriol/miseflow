Main unit or Original unit: what should the cook see?

Every view can show amounts in the kitchen's Main unit (grams, millilitres) or in the Original unit the recipe was written in. This demo answers two questions: how far should the switch reach (everything, each view on its own, or a tap on one amount), and how should scaled and converted amounts be rounded for display (236.6 g, ⅓ cup doubled, 1½ eggs, 2400 g). Nothing here is rounded in the data. The panel on the left always shows the exact value behind what you see.
